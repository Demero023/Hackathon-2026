Why Mojela's exists

Mothers must return to work

Maternity leave ends. Many mothers have no family nearby to look after their baby, and hiring a stranger feels unsafe.

Gogos have skills but no income

Older women in our communities have decades of caregiving experience, but no way to offer it formally or earn from it.

Trust has to come first

Mothers want someone from their own community, who speaks their language and understands their traditions.

How it works

  1. Create your account

    Tell us about your baby — or your experience as a caregiver.

  2. Find a Gogo or Auntie nearby

    Filter by town and see who is available in your area.

  3. Send a care request

    She accepts, you connect on WhatsApp, and you arrange the details together.

Built for our community

  • Local first — Gogos and Aunties in your own town
  • WhatsApp-first — no complicated chat inside the app
  • She chooses you — every request is accepted by the caregiver herself
  • Culture matters — caregivers who speak your language and know your traditions
